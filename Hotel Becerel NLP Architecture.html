Hotel Becerel NLP Architecture
Hotel Becerel · reservation assistant · local, no API key

How the chatbot understands and books

Every message goes through three layers: the Streamlit app collects it, a language-understanding layer turns it into an intent plus stay details, and a dialogue manager decides what to say or do. Nothing is written to the reservations database until the guest confirms.

How accurate it is

Test setClassifier aloneFull pipeline
Held-out intents (60 messages, never trained on)95.0%96.7%
Held-out FAQ questions (37)73.0%*100%
Casual-slang benchmark (96, original notebook set)67.7%100%†

On the casual-slang set, the original notebook model scored 46.9% and the previous version of this classifier 40.6%, abstaining on 42 of 96 messages. Contraction and slang expansion, entity masking, 273 extra varied training examples, and a confidence threshold calibrated with cross-validation raised the classifier alone to 67.7% with 6 abstentions.

* FAQ retriever alone. † The rules were written after inspecting this set, so it is a regression check, not independent evidence. In a hand-graded batch of 35 brand-new questions, 30 were answered well or usefully; the rest got a partial answer, a clarifying question, or occasionally a wrong topic.
